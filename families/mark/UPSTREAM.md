# Upstream: Foundation Logo

`upstream/` is a copy of [rsperberg/foundation-logo](https://github.com/rsperberg/foundation-logo) at commit `703e55b` (22 Nov 2021), trimmed to what the build and licence need: the v0.3 TTF (`fonts/ttf/latest/`, identical to the removed `fonts/ttf/v0.3/` copy), the original iFontMaker source and the licence and font-log files. The sample images and logo reference frames were removed; the images in `upstream/README.md` link to the original repo.
