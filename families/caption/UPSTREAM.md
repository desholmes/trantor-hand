# Upstream: Foundation One

`upstream/` is a copy of [rsperberg/foundation-one](https://github.com/rsperberg/foundation-one) at commit `e21be3f` (22 Nov 2021), trimmed to what the build and licence need: the v0.4 TTF, the original iFontMaker source (v0.42) and the licence and font-log files. The sample images were removed; the images in `upstream/README.md` and `upstream/about-this-font.md` link to the original repo.
